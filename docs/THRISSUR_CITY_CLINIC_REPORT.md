# Thrissur City Private Practice & Clinic Market Report

This report zooms in specifically on **Thrissur City** (encompassing Swaraj Round, Round North, Round South, Chembukkavu, Ayyanthole, Mission Quarters, and Ollur urban belt) and identifies the private practitioners and independent clinics operating without a dedicated custom app or website.

---

## 1. Thrissur City Healthcare Ecosystem
Thrissur City is the medical hub of Central Kerala. While major corporate and trust hospitals (Jubilee Mission Medical College in East Fort, Amala Institute in Thrissur West, Aswini Hospital, and Elite Mission Hospital) dominate inpatient and tertiary care, outpatient (OPD) care is heavily driven by independent private consultation practices.

## 3. Profile of Target Private Practitioners in Thrissur City
*   **Consultant Specialists**: Doctors who work at Jubilee Mission or Amala during daytime hours and operate private evening clinics (typically 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM) in Thrissur City.
*   **Pain Points**:
    *   Patients calling repeatedly during evening hours to check doctor availability or appointment slots.
    *   Managing walk-ins alongside pre-booked phone appointments without double-booking conflicts.
    *   Legibility and retention of handwritten prescription records.
